Privacy note

For parents and carers of children playing My Little Home.

We don't collect anything. Nothing your child does in the game leaves your device.

What the game stores, and where

A few things are saved only in this browser on this device (in its local storage), so the game remembers them next time:

  • the name and boy/girl choice typed on the first screen,
  • sound and volume settings,
  • stickers found, the chosen costume, today's surprise balloon,
  • room settings like lamps and curtains,
  • your child's drawings (the newest four pictures).

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server that receives it.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, logins or sign-ups.
  • No advertising and no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics, tracking, cookies or third-party code.
  • No chat, messaging or links that take children out of the game.
  • No camera, microphone or location access.

Offline use

After the first visit, the game keeps a copy of its own files on the device so it can open without internet. These are only the game's pictures and code, not anything about your child.

Removing everything

Open the grown-ups corner in the game (tap the house logo at the top left) and choose Start over. This deletes the saved name, stickers, drawings and settings from the device. Clearing the browser's site data does the same.

Hosting

The game's files are served by our web host, which, like any website, may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and reliability. The game itself sends no data.

Questions

If you have a question about this note, please contact the person who shared this game with you.
